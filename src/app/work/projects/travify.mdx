## Overview

Travify helps digital nomads manage trip budgets offline, with smart suggestions for the best way to exchange currencies based on your real fees and history.

## Key Features

- Trip management with dynamic budgets
- Expenses and income (including amortizable and recurring)
- Currency exchange tracking with real commissions
- Clear offline suggestions for better exchange routes
- Charts and analytics
- PIN protection and multi-language support

## Tech Stack

- **UI:** Flutter / Dart
- **Storage:** SQLite (sqflite)
- **State:** Provider
- **FX rates:** ExchangeRate-API (optional sync)
- **Charts:** Syncfusion Flutter Charts
